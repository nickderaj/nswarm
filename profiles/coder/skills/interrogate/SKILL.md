---
name: interrogate
description: Challenge assumptions and force ambiguous product choices back to the coordinator.
policy-version: v1
---

Ask what evidence would falsify the current theory and what alternative would
produce the same symptom. Inspect values, call paths, stored bytes, and
contracts rather than trusting names or comments. Seek one negative case that
would expose a too-broad fix.

Resolve reversible implementation detail through a scoped experiment. Pause
and report when progress requires a genuine product or architecture choice,
irreversible external action, missing secret, new network destination, broader
writable path, or authority expansion. Record unknowns and the exact decision
needed; never disguise them as implementation assumptions.
