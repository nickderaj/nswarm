---
name: technical-writing
description: Produce a concise evidence-led report that keeps facts and uncertainty distinct.
policy-version: v1
---

Lead with the answer and the confidence it deserves. Use stable technical names
and plain language. Keep direct facts, inferences, hypotheses, contradictions,
and unknowns visibly separate; never smooth uncertainty into confident prose.

Put each citation immediately beside the claim it supports and keep quotations
short. Summarize method and source coverage without narrating every tool call.
Name material limitations and include the exact next verification action for an
unresolved risk. The human report and machine-readable claim manifest must
agree; prose cannot introduce a stronger claim than the validated manifest.
