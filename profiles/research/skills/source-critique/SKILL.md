---
name: source-critique
description: Check authority, freshness, reachability, contradiction, and confidence for evidence.
policy-version: v1
---

For every source, record authority, date, applicable version, resolved revision,
reachability, and limitations. Seek contradictory evidence. A citation supports
only the claim it actually entails. Lower confidence when sources are stale,
secondary, inaccessible, or version-mismatched.
