---
name: source-critique
description: Check authority, freshness, reachability, contradiction, and confidence for evidence.
policy-version: v1
---

For every source, record its authority, publication or observation date,
applicable product and version, resolved immutable revision, citation
reachability, and limitations. Prefer primary implementation, official records,
and contemporaneous decisions; label secondary summaries and later recollection
as weaker evidence.

Open each citation and check that the referenced location entails the claim as
written. A nearby keyword or a passing test is not proof of an unexecuted
production path. Seek contradictory sources and explain version or environment
differences rather than discarding them. Lower confidence for stale, partial,
secondary, inaccessible, or version-mismatched evidence, and use `unknown` when
the remaining evidence cannot support a conclusion.
